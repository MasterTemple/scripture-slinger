Rules
  • Type the word on an enemy to lock on — every letter hurls a stone.
  • Once locked, you must finish that word.
  • Enter sounds the shofar and brings the walls down.
  • Clear a wave without a typo to earn a life — up to three extra shields.
  • Esc pauses. Each wave is harder than the last.
